Jeroen Gunning

Lawyer in Utrecht · maker of Apps voor Advocaten

I'm Jeroen Gunning, a lawyer in Utrecht at Gunning Sprenger Houben Advocaten. I've been practising law since 2016, focusing on criminal law, victims' rights and juvenile criminal law. Within that, I have a particular interest in cybercrime — cases where law and technology meet.

And it's right at that intersection that these apps are born. Alongside my practice I have a keen interest in technology, and in my day-to-day work I regularly run into small, time-consuming tasks — like putting together unavailable dates for the court. Rather than doing that by hand every time, I build a tool for it.

The apps

Every app you'll find here is meant to make the work of legal practice just a little lighter. Made by a lawyer, for lawyers. They run locally on your own device, with no account or cloud. I also share the occasional tip on the blog about other apps that legal professionals might find useful.
