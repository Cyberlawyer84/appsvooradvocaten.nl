Postcodes and distances,
straight from your menu bar.

Built for criminal defence lawyers: find the postcode of a hearing location, prison or police station in one tap — and, for on-call (piket) cases, the driving distance from your office too. Ready to copy straight into your claim to the Legal Aid Board (Raad voor Rechtsbijstand).

Download on the Mac App Store
macOS 14 or newer · free · fully local
What for

Built for your Legal Aid Board fee claims.

When you claim your fees from the Legal Aid Board (Raad voor Rechtsbijstand) you need the postcode of each location — and, for on-call (piket) cases, the distance from your office to that location too. Afstanden gives you both in seconds, ready to copy straight into Mijn RvR.

Claiming for criminal cases

Instantly look up the right postcode for a (hearing) location and copy it into Mijn RvR.

Claiming for on-call (piket) cases

Get both the postcode and the driving distance from your office in one go — exactly what your on-call claim asks for.

Features

The right postcode and distance, fast.

A lightweight menu-bar app that's always within reach — built for everyday practice.

Driving distance via Apple Maps

The actual driving distance from your office address to every destination, calculated automatically.

Lightning-fast search

Type a name and instantly find the postcode and distance. Look up addresses with Apple Maps-style suggestions.

In your menu bar

Always one click away, without opening a window or interrupting your work.

Your own categories

The app is neutral: you create your own categories, pick icons and add your own locations.

Ready-made lists

Download complete lists of courts, correctional facilities and police stations in one tap.

Private and local

No account, no tracking. Your data stays on your Mac.

Included lists

Ready to download inside the app.

Up-to-date lists you import in one tap. After that, the app recalculates the distances from your own address.
