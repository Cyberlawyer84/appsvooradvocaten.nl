Frequently asked questions

Afstanden — everything you want to know

General

What is Afstanden for?

Afstanden is built for criminal defence lawyers. For your fee claim to the Legal Aid Board (Raad voor Rechtsbijstand) you need the postcode of a location — and, for on-call (piket) cases, the driving distance from your office too. Afstanden gives you both in seconds, ready to copy straight into Mijn RvR.

Which devices is Afstanden for?

Afstanden is an app for the Mac (macOS 14 or newer). There is currently no iPhone or Windows version.

What does the app cost?

Afstanden is free and available in the Mac App Store.

Where do I find the app after installing?

Afstanden runs in your menu bar — the bar at the top of your screen — not as a regular window in the Dock. Click the icon in the menu bar to start searching.

Getting started

How do I get started? The list is still empty.

The first time, the list is empty. Open the settings, enter your office address and download a ready-made list (courts, prisons or police stations), or add your own locations. After that you can start searching right away.

How is the distance calculated?

The app uses Apple Maps to calculate the actual driving distance from your office address to the location. If you change your address, the app recalculates the distances automatically.

How do I search for a location?

Type part of the name; you'll instantly see the postcode and — if you've set an office address — the distance. Copy it with one click.

Can I add my own locations and categories?

Yes. The app is set up to be neutral: you create your own categories, pick icons and add your own locations — alongside or instead of the ready-made lists.

Which lists can I download?

District & appeal courts, prisons/youth/forensic institutions, and police stations. You import them in one tap; after that the app calculates the distances from your own address.

Claiming from the Legal Aid Board

Does the distance exactly match what the Legal Aid Board uses?

Afstanden gives you the driving distance via Apple Maps as a tool to get a good value quickly. Always check your claim yourself; you remain responsible for what you submit to the Legal Aid Board (Raad voor Rechtsbijstand).

Where do the postcodes and lists come from?

The ready-made lists of names, addresses and postcodes are downloaded by the app. The driving distance isn't part of them: the app always works that out itself from your office address, so it's correct for your situation.

Privacy

Where is my data stored?

Your office address, categories and locations stay locally on your Mac. There's no account, and the developer collects or receives no data. Read more in the privacy policy.

Does the app need the internet?

Only to download the ready-made lists and to calculate the driving distance via Apple Maps. Beyond that, the app works entirely locally.
