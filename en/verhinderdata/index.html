Your unavailable dates,
at the press of a button.

Verhinderdata reads your calendar and bundles the days you're unavailable into tidy date ranges — ready to paste into a letter or email. No more manual work.

Download on the App Store
One app for both Mac and iPhone · macOS 14 / iOS 17 or later · fully local and private
Features

Everything you need.

Verhinderdata combines your calendar, your fixed unavailable days and smart bundling into a single overview you can use right away.

Straight from your calendar

Reads your appointments in the chosen period and automatically turns them into unavailable days.

Smart bundling

Consecutive days — including weekends and your fixed unavailable days — are merged into ranges ("3 to 7 March").

Default unavailable days

Set fixed weekdays that always count as unavailable — for example your regular day off.

Filter to your needs

Choose which calendars count, exclude appointments by keyword and decide which recurring activities are included.

Copy or export

One click to copy the overview to your clipboard, or export it as a text file.

Private and local

Your calendar data stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

How it works

Done in three steps.

01

Pick a period

Set a start and end date for which you want to report your unavailable dates.

02

Generate

Verhinderdata fetches your appointments and bundles the unavailable days automatically.

03

Copy or export

Paste the ready-made overview into your letter or email.
